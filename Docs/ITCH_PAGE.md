# itch.io page draft — The Hollow Seven

## Short description
A haunted casino. A dealer who offers you luck. Combine number chips to hit the target — but every number carries a meaning, and the house is listening.

## Pitch
You lost her to another man. At the Hollow Seven, a masked dealer offers you luck that lets you see what she never told you. Win hands by combining chips with + - x / to reach the target. Lucky numbers (3, 7, 8, 9, 77, 108, 777, 786, 888) feed your Luck; cursed ones (4, 13, 17, 49, 666) drain it. Fill the meter to unlock the next memory. Hit the bottom and the house collects.
The in-game Codex explains why each number is lucky or cursed in different cultures.

## Controls
- Mouse: click a chip, click an operator, click a second chip
- Keyboard: 1-6 select chips, Q/W/E/R = + - x /, Z or Backspace = Undo, H = Hint (costs Luck)
- P = pause (works in browser fullscreen); Esc = cancel selection / pause, M = mute, Space or click = continue story (hold Space to skip the opening)
- Right-click = clear selection

### Short controls line for the page
Mouse or 1–6: chips · Q/W/E/R: +/−/×/÷ · Z/Backspace: Undo · H: Hint · P: Pause · M: Mute · Space/click: reveal text or continue. Hold Space to skip the opening. You do not need every chip.
