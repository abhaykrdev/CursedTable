# Hero reaction — voice handoff

The animated 2D shot is implemented immediately after the first breakup vision. GameManager.HeroLaughVoice is the audio slot. The clip plays on entry, obeys Master/SFX/mute, pauses with the game, and stops if the shot is advanced or left.

Please supply an original recording (WAV or OGG preferred) in Assets/Downloads. Suggested name: HeroLaugh.wav. The available Unity audio-generation provider is not configured; no generated or recorded human voice has been added.

Line: LUCK... ahh... NUMBERS.. haha... muhahahahahahah

Direction: a wounded adult man discovering vindictive delight. Begin with an almost disbelieving smile, a quiet breath, then growing, unrestrained laughter. Original voice/performance; no sampled anime audio.

Timing target, about 9 seconds:
- 0.0–2.2: LUCK... (quiet, savoring the word)
- 2.2–3.3: ahh...
- 3.3–4.8: NUMBERS..
- 4.8–5.5: haha...
- 5.5–9.0: muhahahahahahah, rising then cutting off

The timing can be adjusted to the actual recording. Current mouth movement is stylized animation, not phoneme-driven lip sync.
